Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. Draft: have this reviewed by your legal adviser before launch.

Chekd Cars ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website and how we use it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

When you book a doorstep visit, ask for an offer, request a Chekd Passport, join a town waitlist or send a partnership enquiry, we collect what you type into the form: your name, phone number, address or town, and vehicle details such as registration number and kilometres.

Why we use it

Only to respond to your request: to call or WhatsApp you, schedule a visit, inspect your vehicle, make an offer, issue your Chekd Passport, or discuss a partnership. We don't sell your data.

Consent

By sending a form you consent to this use. You can withdraw consent at any time by messaging us, and we will stop processing and delete your data unless the law requires us to keep it.

Who we share it with

Our staff and Chekd Captains who handle your request, and service providers that host our systems (our database runs on Supabase). Partner outlets see only the requests for their town.

How long we keep it

As long as needed for your request and any vehicle history you asked us to keep, or as required by law (for example, tax records).

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your data, or nominate someone to act for you. Contact our grievance officer on WhatsApp at +91 90190 88160. We reply within 7 days.
